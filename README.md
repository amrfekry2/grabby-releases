## What it does

- **Faster downloads.** Each file downloads over several connections at once.
- **Pause and resume.** Stop a download and pick it up later, even after closing Grabby or losing your connection.
- **Videos from the page you are watching.** On YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Vimeo and many other sites, a **Grab video** button appears beside your browser. Choose a quality and the download starts.
- **Copy a link, Grabby offers to download it.** No extension needed.
- **Queue, schedule, and shut down when finished.**
- **Light and dark themes.** No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/amrfekry2/grabby-releases/releases/latest).
2. Download the file that ends in **`x64-setup.exe`**.
3. Run it. Grabby installs for your user only, so it needs no administrator rights.

Grabby keeps itself up to date after that.

**Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"?** Grabby is new, so Windows does not recognise it yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This warning goes away as more people install Grabby.

Requires Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

## Privacy

Grabby does not collect, store or send any information about you or what you download. Your download list and settings stay on your PC. Grabby connects only to the sites you download from, and to GitHub to check for updates.
